## R01 - Balance does not match its components

The balance on an account line must equal premium minus commission minus claims paid, within 0.01. Lines with a missing amount are left to R08.

Tolerances: `tolerance` = 0.01

## R02 - Commission rate differs from the treaty rate

Commission divided by premium must match the treaty commission rate within 0.5 percentage points.

Tolerances: `rate_tolerance` = 0.005

## R03 - Period lies outside the treaty term

The account period must fall entirely inside the term of the treaty it is booked against.

## R04 - Duplicate account line

The same treaty, period and amounts must not be reported twice. The earliest submission is kept; every later copy is flagged.

## R05 - Currency differs from the treaty currency

The currency on an account line must equal the currency of its treaty.

## R06 - Treaty is not in the master data

The treaty reference on an account line must exist in the treaty master data.

## R07 - Negative premium not marked as reversal

A negative premium is only valid when the line is flagged as a reversal.

## R08 - Required field is missing

Treaty, period, currency, premium, commission, claims paid and balance must all be present.

## R09 - Period ends after the submission date

A cedent cannot report a period that has not ended by the date it submitted the account.
